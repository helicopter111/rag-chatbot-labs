# Harbor & Pine Coffee — Store Handbook

Welcome to Harbor & Pine. This handbook is the source of truth for baristas
and shift leads at the Maple Street shop. If a policy is not written here,
ask a manager — do not invent an answer.

## Hours and last call

The shop is open 6:30am to 6:00pm Monday through Friday, and 8:00am to 4:00pm
on Saturday and Sunday. Last call for espresso drinks is 15 minutes before
close. Drip coffee may be poured until 5 minutes before close.

## Espresso standard

Our house espresso is a 18 gram dose in, 36 gram yield out, in 25 to 30
seconds. If a shot runs faster than 22 seconds or slower than 32 seconds,
discard it and adjust the grind. Milk drinks use whole milk unless the guest
asks otherwise. Oat milk is the default dairy-free option.

## Employee drinks and discount

After you have been on shift for 2 hours you may make one employee drink.
Employee drinks are not allowed in the last 30 minutes of a shift. The
employee discount is 40 percent off food and drinks for the working employee
only. Friends and family do not receive the discount unless a manager
approves a one-time exception.

## Time off

New hires receive 15 days of paid time off after 90 days of employment.
Requests must be submitted in the scheduling app at least 14 days ahead.
Blackout dates include the week of the Maple Street Festival (second week of
August) and the two days before Thanksgiving.

## Lost and found

Items left in the shop go in the lost-and-found bin under the pastry case.
We keep them for 14 days. Wallets and keys are given to the shift lead and
logged in the binder — they are never left in the bin.

## Wi-Fi and passwords

Guest Wi-Fi is `HarborPine-Guest`. The password is printed on the receipt and
on a card at the register. Staff network passwords live in the manager
binder. Never put staff passwords into the chatbot, Slack, or email.
